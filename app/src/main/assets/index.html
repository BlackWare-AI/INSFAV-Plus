Clearing the index or image cache never deletes your photos or collections. Rescan deletes direct WebP thumbnails absent from a validated, nonempty Favorites list when write access is granted. The optional fetcher writes only validated profile thumbnails.

    Workers

    Per-worker delay and working hours

    Each WebView has its own start-to-start delay. A waiting worker does not block other ready workers. Server cooldown and access restrictions apply to the whole pool. Batch work follows these hours, including overnight windows. Viewer requests take priority after active operations finish and still obey delays and access restrictions. Android may delay background work.
